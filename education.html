Education & Skills

Courses, school tools and professional learning

Education searches include very different needs: a student looking for a course, a school buying management software, or a professional comparing certification programs. SEEKVERA keeps those intents separate.

What to check

  • Check who issues the certificate and whether it matters for your goal.
  • Compare course depth, projects and support — not only duration.
  • Schools should check data export and parent communication features.
  • Avoid promises of guaranteed jobs or admissions.
