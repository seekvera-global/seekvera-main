Connectivity

Find free / public Wi-Fi nearby.

With your permission, SEEKVERA uses your phone location to look for nearby places in OpenStreetMap data that are tagged with Wi-Fi or internet access. Availability is community-supplied and may not always be current.

🗺️ Search on Maps
Tap “Find Wi-Fi around me” to search within about 5 km.

Use public Wi-Fi carefully

  • Prefer networks provided by the venue and confirm the exact network name with staff.
  • Avoid banking, passwords or sensitive work on unknown public networks when possible.
  • Keep HTTPS enabled; a trusted VPN can add protection on untrusted networks.
  • Turn off automatic Wi-Fi joining and forget the network when you are finished.
